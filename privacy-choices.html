Privacy Choices

There's nothing to opt out of — here's why, and how to reach us anyway.

Effective 2026-08-16 judysu1983@icloud.com

The short version

  • We never sell or share your personal information with third parties for advertising.
  • There's no targeted advertising, no ad identifiers, and no cross-app or cross-site tracking to opt out of in the first place.
  • Laws like the CCPA and GDPR give you rights around data sale, sharing, and profiling — A+ Farsi does none of those things.
  • Want details on what little we do collect, or a question answered? Email us — see below.

Why there's nothing to configure here

Privacy-choice pages usually exist to let you opt out of things like the sale of your personal information, "sharing" for cross-context behavioral advertising, or targeted ads built from a profile of your activity. A+ Farsi doesn't do any of that: there's no advertising in the app at all, no ad network, no advertising identifiers, and no cross-app or cross-site tracking. We don't build behavioral profiles, and we've never sold or shared personal information with anyone.

Because none of the activity these choice mechanisms exist to control is happening, there's no toggle or opt-out form to offer — the honest answer is that the choice is already made, by design, for everyone.

What we do collect (and your rights over it)

The only things A+ Farsi collects are described in full in the Privacy Policy: anonymous, aggregate usage events keyed by a random on-device identifier, and short voice clips processed in the moment to score pronunciation. Neither is linked to your identity, sold, or shared for advertising.
