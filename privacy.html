Privacy Policy

What A+ Farsi collects, why, and what stays on your child's device.

Effective 2026-08-16 judysu1983@icloud.com

The short version

  • No accounts, no sign-up — we never ask for an email, phone number, or password.
  • No ads, no cross-app tracking, no advertising identifiers — nothing here follows you elsewhere.
  • A child's name, avatar, and progress stay on the device — never sent to us.
  • We collect only anonymous usage statistics and process voice recordings solely to score pronunciation and generate spoken examples.

What we collect, and why

Voice recordings — only while practicing pronunciation. When a child taps the microphone to practice speaking Farsi, the app records a short clip and sends it to our server, which forwards it to our speech provider, OpenAI, to transcribe it (so we can score the pronunciation) and to generate spoken word examples. We do not store these recordings to build a profile. Under our provider's API terms, audio is not used to train its models and is retained only briefly, if at all, for abuse monitoring, then deleted. Audio is tied to a request, not to a child's identity.

Anonymous usage statistics. To understand which features help kids learn, the app sends aggregate, anonymous events — for example "app opened" or "viewed the Alphabet screen." These are keyed by a random identifier created on the device — not your name, not your Apple ID, and not a device or advertising ID. We also receive the platform and OS version. This data is not linked to any identity and not used to track you.

Information stored only on the device (not collected by us). The profile name and avatar you choose, your learning progress, and your language setting are saved locally and never transmitted off the device. Deleting a profile or the app removes all of it.

Illustrations. Most word illustrations are bundled inside the app itself and load with no network request. A smaller set of everyday-object photos are requested from Wikimedia Commons at view time — as with any web image, that reveals your device's IP address to that host. We never send Wikimedia any personal information alongside the request.

Children's privacy

A+ Farsi is built for children and families. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children beyond the anonymous, functional data described above, and we never condition play on providing personal information. If you're a parent or guardian and believe we've collected personal information from your child that we shouldn't have, contact us at judysu1983@icloud.com and we'll delete it.

Sharing, retention & security

We do not sell or rent data. We share data only with the infrastructure providers that operate the app on our behalf — Google Cloud (hosts our server and anonymous-analytics storage) and OpenAI (speech transcription and synthesis) — acting as processors for those functions only.

Anonymous usage events are retained for up to 14 months, then deleted or aggregated. We do not retain voice recordings; our speech provider's brief retention is described above. Requests are sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS), and our API key is held only on our server — never inside the app.

Your choices & changes

Because we don't hold an account for you, the on-device data (name, avatar, progress) is fully under your control — delete the app to remove it. See Privacy Choices for more on data rights. For questions about anonymous analytics, or to request deletion of data tied to your device's random identifier, contact judysu1983@icloud.com. We may update this policy; we'll change the effective date above and note material changes in the app or store listing.
